# Shrub forms

Five G107 profile recipes and a specimen row, plus a multi-colony blackberry thicket,
an all-dead cane asset and sparse sagebrush fitted to a local 0.3 rise/run plane.
[Grounding](../../../docs/experiments/shrub-growth-forms.md) and
[solver contract](../../../docs/subsystems/flora.md#the-shrub).

`scene.json` presents the five default forms at their own sizes. `camera-path.json` moves
from the row to a closer view. Cook/capture with the worktree's `engine-view --scene ...
--offscreen --capture <outside-checkout.png>`, or cook individual `.flora.json` recipes with
`engine-content flora`. Material roles are the named species plus `/bark` and `/leaf`.
The checked-in scene needs no external textures; it has no ground mesh.

Generated containers, captures, camera/recipe copies and measurements belong under
`D:/workspace/game_engine_local/shrubs/<date>/`, never here. The sparse slope recipe needs
matching ground supplied by the consumer; it is not included in the flat specimen row.
The all-dead asset uses geometric LOD, because it has no foliage to carry the far-chain contract.
Visual review includes matched 5, 20 and 60 m views and a view beyond the measured far-form handover.
