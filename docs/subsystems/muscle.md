# muscle (systems)

**Purpose.** The line tier of the engine's muscles: a Hill-type **line actuator** on a routed path — activation dynamics, a fiber with active, passive and damping force, and a compliant tendon in series at a fixed pennation height — integrated at the scheduler's 60 Hz tick. A game drives up to 200 of them per character. Stage 1 of the capability ([roadmap](../roadmap.md) C53) is tier 0 (near characters) as a batch kernel — the law, the scheme, the located slack/taut crossing, the whole-tick commit or rollback, the work ledger, static initialisation, the validator, the schema, the qualification corpus replayed as data, and the bench. Stage 2 (C54) adds **the supervisor** ("The supervisor" below): which tier an actuator is in, degrading to a rigid tendon after repeated failure, the inert animation tier, freeze accounting and promotion. This module stays ECS-free, so its corpus and supervisor tests run in the no-ECS build; the scheduler entry, the components' registration and the path's source in the skeleton are a second capability's, `systems/muscle_sim`. It does not do active volume (the tissue program's), and it owns passive fiber, damping and tendon force exclusively (D20).

**Status.** Landed: the schema, the law (`law.h`), the adopted scheme and its S probe (`scheme.h`), the schedule and the locator (`schedule.h`), the store, the tick, static initialisation, save and restore (`store.h`), the corpus replay (`content/muscle/`), the validator (`validator.h`), the safeguarded-Newton stage root (`newton.h`) and the bench, with their tests: stage 1 is complete (roadmap C53, done). Stage 2's supervisor (`supervisor.h`) has landed with its tests against the authoring track's r008 witnesses (C54).

**Why this shape.** The law and the scheme were not designed here. An authoring track (`astra_muscle`, the `06-muscle-activation` worktree) qualified them offline over ten revisions against a held-control Radau reference, under decisions D20–D23 that the engine owner confirmed, and those decisions are the contract ([ADR-0055](../adr/0055-the-muscle-line-tier-scheme-schedule-and-failure-semantics.md)): the r005 reference law, Alexander's SDIRK3 with the located-crossing locator (adopted at r009), the r010 schedule (four pieces per parent on the first tick, four remainder steps after a crossing), whole-tick rollback, the corpus as the only qualified domain. The engine's work is to carry them exactly and make them cheap and deterministic, not to improve them: a change of scheme, substep count or gate is a new authoring declaration, not an engine edit. Three engine choices sit on top. **Deterministic transcendentals** ([math](math.md), `engine::det`): D20 requires the committed state to be the same bytes on MSVC, GCC and Clang, and the C library's `exp` and `tanh` are not. **The path is a template parameter** of the tick, so a sample is an inlined call, never an indirect one per stage. **A failure is a value, not an exception**: every function that can fail returns `false` with the enumerated `ValidationTest` and the value it read, so a failed tick costs a branch and retains the committed bytes by never having written them.

**Owned data.** The committed line state of every actuator in a store: `(last_commit_tick, activation, fiber_length, startup_pending, the force cache)` with the identities (actuator, parameter set, path, model), the supervisory half of the record (`Supervisory`: the tier, the pending transition, the frozen and rigid streaks, the stale flag, the last attempted tick, the last path length), the per-actuator law rows derived from the parameters, and each actuator's fixed per-tick slots (its outcome, counters, `TickWork` and four `ValidationEvent`s). Nothing else holds or mutates them.

**Invariants** (each tested; the files are under `systems/muscle/tests/`):
- The law reproduces the reference's forces, energies and residual at its archived initial states to 1e-9 relative (`law_tests.cpp`, the four embedded rows; `corpus_tests.cpp`, all 39).
- Every corpus case passes the D20 gates its class requires, a batch of all 39 equals each alone byte for byte, and the final states' hash is the pinned one; every corpus number round-trips through `core/json` (`corpus_tests.cpp`, "The corpus" below).
- The validator's qualified domain is the corpus's 17 identities, every corpus case validates, and each refusal names its code, field, value, unit and rule; a refused actuator is never added (`validator_tests.cpp`).
- The tableau is r009's pinned binary64 Alexander SDIRK3; its rational forms and the order-three conditions hold; the shared stage loop is third order on `y' = -y` with `y + w` conserved, and its stability function is `R(z)` with `R -> 0` in the stiff limit (`scheme_tests.cpp`).
- No `std::` transcendental is named in the kernel's sources (`law_tests.cpp` greps them).
- The schedule is fixed: 32 probes on a startup tick and 8 after, whatever the probe returns; parents restart at their canonical times (`schedule_tests.cpp`).

## The law, in the engine's words

The authoring track's r005 law, carried expression for expression (`law.h`). Its canonical text is `systems/muscle/tests/model.txt`, one formula or constant per line; the engine's model identity is that file's SHA-256 (`0238cd30…`), computed once with `Get-FileHash` and pinned as `k_model_sha256`, beside the reference's `3096e5ff…`, the identity of the law it implements.

- **Activation**: `da/dt = (u - a)((s + 1/2)/(tau_act z) + (1/2 - s) z / tau_deact)`, `z = 1/2 + 3a/2`, `s = tanh(10 (u - a))/2`; each stage's activation is a classical RK4 step from the probe's starting activation over `c_i d`, the excitation held. The three RK4 steps share their first slope, which is the same value each time.
- **Fiber**: `x = l / lopt` snapped to an endpoint within 4 ulp of it, else in `[0.5, 1.6]`; three Gaussians for active force-length; `asinh` force-velocity with `w = v / vmax` in `[-1, 1]` (a value in `(-1e-14, 0)` is zero); an exponential passive curve and its energy; damping `F0 beta v / vmax`.
- **Series**: a fixed height `lopt sin(alpha0)`; the belly's projection `sqrt(l^2 - h^2)` with a cosine floor; a tendon that is zero in compression and `0.2 F0 expm1(k strain)` in tension, `k = ln 6 / e1`, with `k strain > 100` a domain error; the residual `(F_a + F_p + F_d) cos - F_t`.
- **The implicit stage**: the fiber velocity whose length `base + gamma d v` zeroes the residual, bracketed by the length domain and the trial-extension cap (`L > Ls (1 + 2 e1)`) and by `[-vmax, vmax]`, the bracket moved one `nextafter` inward, solved by Brent exactly as scipy's `brentq` iterates (`xtol 2e-14`, `rtol 1e-14`, 64 iterations) or, the kernel's choice, by a **safeguarded Newton inside the same bracket** (`newton.h`, D23 (5)): the same residual with its slope along `l = base + gamma d v` from the residual's own exponentials, the Newton step when it lands strictly inside the bracket the residuals' signs keep shrinking and a bisection otherwise, from the bracket's secant point, stopping on a step under Brent's tolerance. Under the corpus gates the two agree to the four digits the replay prints, both give the same bytes on the three compilers, and Newton costs 20–23% less (fewer residuals: 4.5–6.3 iterations a stage against 8.0–9.6); `k_stage_root` is Newton, and the corpus test runs and pins both. "Empty bracket", "not bracketed" and "not converged" are failures either way.
- **The S probe** (`probe_s`): three stages, `base_i = l + d sum_{j<i} a_ij v_j` in increasing `j`, the end state `(t + d, a_3, l_3)`, stage 3's components the cache; the five work channels (`external = F_t dL/dt`, `active_output = -F_a v`, `dissipation = F_d v`, and the two absolute values) gain `(d b_i) P_i` in stage order with the signed weights kept (`b_2 < 0`), nothing clipped.

**Provenance**, file by file, all under `06-muscle-activation` (read as text; nothing there was run): `r005/model.py` (the fiber side), `r005/compliant.py` (the series side and `static_initial_length`), `r005/stepping.py` (`stage`, `sample_path`, `validate_state`), `r009/declarations/d001/ARM-S.md` and `TABLEAU.json` (the scheme), `r009/execution/shared/methods.py` (`stage_loop`, `probe_s`, `locate`), `r008/execution/a001/arm_a.py` (`gap`, `gap_rate`), `r010/declarations/d001/SCHEDULE.md`, `LOCATOR.md`, `BUDGET.md`, `METHOD.json` (the schedule), `r007/study.py` (the corpus's control and path kinds, the ledger), `r007/STATE-CONTRACT.md` and `r008/declarations/d001/muscle.schema` (the schema's proposal), and the decisions D20–D23 with the engine owner's four reviews.

## The schedule and the located crossing

`schedule.h`, r010's `SCHEDULE.md` and `LOCATOR.md` in the engine's words. Tick `k` advances `[(k-1)/60, k/60]` through eight **parents** of `H = 1/480 s` (`0x1.1111111111111p-9`). Parent `j` starts at its canonical time `((k-1) + j/8)/60` with the state carried from the parent before: the time is reset, never accumulated across parents. While `startup_pending` is set, a parent is four **pieces** of `h = H/4` (`0x1.1111111111111p-11`); otherwise one piece of `h = H`. A piece is one S probe from its incoming state; within a parent, pieces and remainder steps carry their propagated times.

After each probe the gap `g = L - projected(l) - Ls` is read at the start, both internal stages and the end. The start's phase is the sign of its gap, or at an exact zero the sign of the outgoing gap rate `dL/dt - v_eq / cos` (the equilibrium velocity's root), cached by the complete state; at most two such queries per parent, a zero or nonfinite rate `EventTangentUnresolved`, a third query `EventBudgetExceeded` before it is evaluated (r010's rule). An end of the start's phase with an internal stage of the other is `EventUnresolved`, a zero internal stage `EventTangentUnresolved`; an end of the other phase or an exact zero is a detected crossing. A detected crossing is located by 20 dyadic bisections of `tau` on the piece's **original-start** one-step map — the low side never advanced, the high side's probe kept, every candidate inspected — and must end with width `<= h/2^20` and `|g_high| <= 1e-8 m`, else `EventLocationUnresolved` (at 1 m/s the bracket leaves 2e-9 m; at 100 m/s 1.3e-7 m, refused: the two bounds agree up to about 5 m/s, and a faster path is a declared failure). The crossing's remainder `h - tau`, if positive, is **four equal S steps** of `r/4` from the event's time, each inspected as a remainder: any crossing or zero there is `EventBudgetExceeded`. **One crossing per parent**: a later piece of the same parent runs one full probe and is inspected the same way, so a second crossing is refused before any search. The ceilings fall out — 28 probes in a startup parent, 25 otherwise, 224 and 200 a tick — and an assertion holds the schedule to them. The located `tau` is a root of the one-step map, not the exact continuous crossing (the owner's r008 review, note (a)).

The schedule is a template over a *prober* — `validate`, `probe`, `gap`, `gap_rate` — so the tests drive it with fixtures whose crossings are known (a gap that is a function of time, or a script keyed by probe index for a second crossing at an exact remainder position) and the kernel with the law (`LineProber`), with no indirect call either way. The tick begins with the reference's `validate_state`: a finite time, the activation in range, the geometry, the path sample, and a committed state that admits the continuous model's equilibrium velocity.

## Commit or roll back, whole tick

A tick is a trial on locals. Success installs activation, fiber length, the cache (the last segment's final stage), `last_commit_tick = k` and `startup_pending = false`, and reports the tick's five work channels. Any failure anywhere — a domain test, a root not bracketed or not converged, an event code, `NotInitialized`, `TickOrder` — retains the committed record bit for bit, reports zero work and the enumerated test with the value it read, and the next tick retries from the retained state with its new inputs. There is no retry inside a tick, no clamp and no fabricated state (D20). Two places where the engine reports differently from the reference without changing what fails: a location failure's diagnostic gap rate, which the reference evaluated for its event record before raising, is not evaluated (it could replace the location code with a root failure of its own); and a tick at or before the last committed one, which the reference raised outside its rollback, is a structured `TickOrder` failure.

## The store and the tick

`store.h`: a structure-of-arrays `LineStore`, sized once by `reserve(capacity)` and never again (`add` refuses past it). The hot row a tick reads per actuator is the `LawRow` (28 doubles derived once from the parameters), with the activation, fiber length, last committed tick, startup marker and cache beside it; identities and stage 2's supervisory fields are cold arrays; each actuator has fixed slots for its last tick's outcome and counters. `tick<Path>(store, k, excitation, paths, samples)` runs every actuator; nothing in it allocates — the stage states, probe records (240 bytes each), the tick's work and the parent samples live on the stack. No early exit cannot be expressed as a mask: an actuator's failure ends that actuator's trial, which a later 4-wide pass across actuators would carry as a lane mask. `static_initial_length` is the reference's (the bracket and cap rule, `nextafter`, Brent with `xtol = rtol = 1e-14`, the restoring-slope check at ±1e-7 m) and `initialize_static` is the only way to arm `startup_pending`; `save` and `restore` move the committed record (`LineState`) in and out, keeping the marker as saved and refusing a record of another law or another parameter set. `tick_actuator` is one actuator's tier-0 tick, which `tick` loops over and the supervisor calls; the split moved no arithmetic, and the corpus's pinned hashes did not move.

## The supervisor

`supervisor.h`, D20 and D21 (4) in the engine's words. `supervise<Path>(store, k, excitation, paths)` is the runtime's tick: per actuator it samples the path at the tick's two ends, applies at the tick's **start** whatever the last tick decided, integrates in the actuator's tier, and decides at the tick's **end**. Three tiers (`LineTier`):

| Tier | A tick | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `Compliant` (0) | the stage-1 kernel, `tick_actuator` | the committed cache's tendon force, stale after a failure |
| `Rigid` (1) | the activation over the tick's eight parents; the fiber at the path's rigid projection at the tick's end; its velocity the projection's change over the tick; the force `(F_a + F_p + F_d) cos` from the law's curves at that state (`rigid_cache`) — no tendon compliance, no implicit root | that force |
| `Animation` (2) | nothing integrates; the projection is checked | none (`output_force` is zero, `has_force_output` false) |

**A frozen tier-0 tick** (any kernel failure) retains the committed mechanics and cache bit for bit, sets `forces_stale`, increments `consecutive_frozen_ticks`, emits a `DomainFailure` or `RootFailure` with the enumerated test and the value it read (a root, locator or budget test is a root failure; the law's domain, the path and the tick order are domain failures), books the freeze ledger, and outputs the stale cached force: no catch-up, and the next tick retries from the retained state with its own inputs. **The eighth** also emits `TierDegraded` with `pending_transition = ToRigid`, the tier still compliant and the stale force still the output. **At the next tick's start** the projection: `belly = L - Ls` at the tick's first instant, `l = sqrt(belly^2 + h^2)`, held to the law's geometry (the normalized-length domain, the pennation height, the cosine floor), with `RigidProjectionInvalid` (appended to `ValidationTest` as 30) for a belly that is not positive. A valid projection installs `l` and the rigid cache at zero fiber velocity, books the jump `E_after - E_before` (the passive energy's change plus the discarded tendon energy) on `TierChanged` and on the tick's `TickWork::transition_elastic_jump`, and the tick integrates in tier 1; an invalid one makes the actuator inert with `TierInert` naming the test.

**Tier 1** is one step and never a silent one: a failure (the excitation's or the activation's range, the fiber velocity's domain, the tick order) retains the record, sets the stale flag, resets the rigid streak, emits the event and books the ledger with the rigid tendon's zero elastic change; a path whose end leaves the rigid geometry makes the actuator inert at once with `TierInert`, since a rigid fiber has no length to hold there. **Sixty consecutive successes** (the first integrated rigid tick is success one; the streak saturates at 60) set `pending_transition = ToCompliant` and emit `PromotionScheduled` at the tick's end; at the next tick's start `initialize_static` (static_initial_length at the retained activation and the path's current length, zero fiber velocity) either installs a static start — `startup_pending` armed, as D24 requires — with `TierPromoted` and the jump booked, and the tick runs compliant with both streaks at zero; or leaves the actuator as it was with `PromotionRejected` naming the static test, the streak at zero, and the tick runs rigid. **An inert tick** retains activation and fiber length, integrates nothing, outputs nothing and books `FreezeAccountingStatus::Inert` (zero external work, valid, and no conservation claimed); at each inert tick's start the projection is tried again, and a valid one resumes tier 1 with `TierResumed` and the jump from the last committed elastic energy to the projected state's, and that tick integrates one nominal rigid tick from the held excitation.

**The freeze ledger** of a frozen tick (`TickWork`): with the fiber held at the retained `l_f` while the path moves from `L_start` to `L_end`, `freeze_external_input = F_stale (L_end - L_start)`, valid only when both ends are finite; `freeze_elastic_change = U_t(L_end - projected(l_f)) - U_t(L_start - projected(l_f))`; `freeze_approximation_error` their signed difference; `resume_initial_elastic` the passive energy at `l_f` plus the tendon energy at `L_end` — the kinematic energy the resume starts from, not the old cache's. None of it enters an integration residual. A path whose ends are not finite, or whose tendon energy at the held fiber is outside the law's domain, is `UndefinedPath` with its own `FreezeAccountingUndefined` event, and the error field is not a claim (the status says so); the tick itself is the kernel's `DomainFailure` (`PathSampleInvalid`).

**Choices the decisions left, and what was chosen.** (1) *Tier 1's activation*: one RK4 step of 1/60 s leaves the activation's domain on an ordinary step from rest (its first half-step reaches `a = 1.11` for `u = 1`, and overshoots below zero for `u = 0.8`), so tier 1 integrates the activation in the schedule's eight parents of H, one RK4 step each: exactly tier 0's activation in a parent with no crossing and no startup pieces, which the test holds to the bit. (2) *Tier 1's ledger*: the five channels by the one-point rule at the tick's end state (`F_t ΔL`, `-F_a v dt`, `F_d v dt`); first order, and not a qualified ledger, since the corpus qualified tier 0 only. (3) *An invalid projection in tier 1* is the inert tier at once rather than a counted rigid failure, by D20's own rule for an invalid projection; a rigid failure otherwise has no degradation beyond resetting the promotion streak. (4) *`consecutive_frozen_ticks`* is tier 0's streak: it keeps the 8 that degraded the actuator through tiers 1 and 2 (the r008 witness's value) and is reset by a promotion and by a committed tier-0 tick. (5) *A promotion's committed tick*: `initialize_static` records the tick before the promoting one, so the promoted tick is the next in order; a promotion asked of a tick at or before the last commit waits rather than moving the committed tick back. (6) `ProjectionFailure` is not emitted: an invalid projection's event is `TierInert` with the test, and an inert tick whose projection is still invalid emits nothing, so an inert actuator does not report every tick. (7) An actuator never initialised reports `NotInitialized` each tick and does not count toward degradation.

**Events and work go to fixed slots.** Each actuator has one `TickWork` and `k_event_slots` = 4 `ValidationEvent`s in the store, which the supervised tick overwrites and a caller drains after it (`tick_events(store, i)`): at most one event at the tick's start (`TierChanged`, `TierInert`, `TierResumed`, `TierPromoted`, `PromotionRejected`), one failure, one `FreezeAccountingUndefined` and one decision at the end (`TierDegraded`, `PromotionScheduled`). Each event carries the identities, the tick's inputs (the excitation and the path's length and rate at the tick's start, `path_inputs_finite`), the test and value, and the supervisory record as it stands after the change it reports. `TickWork::transition_elastic_jump` is the tick's sum and, with at most one transition a tick, equals the event's. **Persistence** is the stage-1 `LineState`, which already held every supervisory field: a restore at any point reproduces the next tick's bytes, the per-tick slots excepted, which the next tick rewrites.

## The corpus

[`content/muscle/`](../../content/muscle/README.md) holds the trajectories the authoring track qualified against, written by `tools/muscle-corpus.ps1` from the **source packets `content/muscle/sources.json` names** (roadmap C55, 2026-10-15: each packet's files with their roles — `cases`, `samples`, `labels` — and the SHA-256 each must have, the qualified domain id and version its rows belong to, and its labels), with every number copied as its source token. Today there is one packet, r010's `CASES.json` and r007's handoff `corpus.json` with r009 S's and r010's summaries under D25's labels (the review of r010's result, 2026-10-14): 39 cases. The script stops on a source with other bytes, and re-derives the 117 per-row hashes and the 39 parameter identities (r012's canonical form of the nineteen values) the sources carry, stopping on any difference; `corpus.json` records every case's domain, identity and provenance (packet, file, row) and every domain's identities with their full parameter sets, and the case count is the sources', never a constant. Until C55 the importer took exactly those two files and 39 rows and the test read exactly three path kinds, which a named muscle's packet could not pass. `corpus_tests.cpp` replays every case from its **archived** static start (the engine's own `static_initial_length` agreeing with it within 1e-9 m first, and the law's outputs there within 1e-9 relative), `startup_pending` set, for its own ticks (`end_s` at 60 Hz: 36, and 24 for `shortening` and `lengthening`, which run 0.4 s; the oracle must have a sample at every parent end of them), the excitation sampled at each tick's start and held, the path the corpus's constant, linear or sine (`AnalyticPath`, through `engine::det`) or **tabulated**: `{"kind": "tabulated", "tick_hz": 60, "samples": [[length_m, rate_m_s], ...]}`, length and rate at every tick boundary from t = 0, the tick count the samples' less one, replayed through the runtime's own `HermitePath` per tick, which is the shape a routed trajectory from a named muscle arrives in. **The tabulated kind is proved on a synthetic case**: `active_wave` tabulated at its 37 boundaries from its analytic path, written as that JSON, read back and replayed (2026-10-15, MSVC, both stage roots the same) passes the three gates against the archived oracle (max |F - oracle| 0.1213 N against the analytic replay's 0.1138, |R| / limit 0.0066 against 0.0065), and differs from the analytic replay by at most 5.2e-2 N and 2.1e-7 m. What to expect is the Hermite's own error: a cubic Hermite through exact values and rates misses a sine of amplitude A and frequency w by at most e_L = A (w h)^4 / 384 at mid-tick (2.405e-7 m here; measured 2.385e-7) and its rate by sqrt(3) / 216 A w^4 h^3 (4.44e-5 m/s; measured 4.37e-5), and the tendon in series turns a length error into at most its stiffness at the run's largest force times it (676,229 N/m, so 0.163 N) while the fiber can take up at most e_L / cos (1.2e-6 m at the minimum cosine); the test holds each to twice its bound. The gates are D20's against the held oracle at t = 0 and every parent endpoint: max |F_t - oracle| <= `force_N`, max |l - oracle| <= `fiber_m`, and the final |R| <= `work_J` = 1e-3 D with `R = W_ext + W_active_output - (E_end - E_0) - W_diss` and `E` the passive fiber plus tendon energy. An unlabelled case must pass all three; a labelled case the two it is labelled as passing, with its failing metric at most 1.5 times r009 S's number. The test also runs all 39 in one store and requires each actuator's final bytes to equal its run alone, and pins a 64-bit hash (`core/hash`) of every case's final activation, fiber length and five work channels for each stage root, taken on MSVC and reproduced by GCC 13 (release) and Clang 18 (debug) in the Linux containers.

## The validator

`validator.h` (D21 (3), from the capability's first version): `validate(model_id, parameter_identity, fiber, series)` refuses, in this order, a model id that is not this law's (`model_unrecognized`; the reference's `3096e5ff…` is the law this one implements, not this law, and is refused too), a parameter-set identity outside the qualified domain or parameters other than the ones that identity was qualified with (`domain_unqualified`), and a value outside the law's own domain (`parameter_outside_domain`: every fiber value finite and positive, `x0 < x_min < x_max`, the series lengths, strain, damping and minimum cosine finite and positive, the minimum cosine below one, the pennation in `[0, 0.4]` rad — the reference's `Parameters` and `SeriesParameters` checks). A refusal names the field, the value, its unit and the rule. **The qualified domains are data** (roadmap C55, 2026-10-15): `qualified_domains()` is every domain of `content/muscle/corpus.json`, each an id, a version and its identities with the **full nineteen-value parameter set** each was qualified with, compiled in from `src/qualified_domains.inc`, which `tools/muscle-corpus.ps1` generates from the same sources as the corpus (never edited by hand; every value a hexadecimal floating literal of its source token's binary64, which a compiler reads with no rounding, the token beside it). `validator_tests.cpp` holds the table to the corpus bit for bit — the same domains and identities in order, all 323 values equal to core/json's reading of the corpus's tokens — so the two cannot drift. Today there is one domain, `synthetic-study-only` version 1, the 17 distinct identities of the 39 rows. The identities are opaque 32-byte values the engine carries and never recomputes — the engine has no SHA-256 outside the tissue capability and a capability does not depend on another for a hash — so the check runs the other way round: an identity names one parameter set, and parameters that differ from it in **any of the nineteen values**, bit for bit, are not qualified by it. Until C55 only the five series values were compared and the fourteen fiber values were compared to the law's defaults, which every synthetic row has and no named muscle would. A `domain_unqualified` refusal of a value names the first field that differs (`fiber.passive_shape`), its value and unit, and the domain and version the identity was found in (`ValidationResult::domain`, `domain_version`, also set on an accepted set); an identity in no domain is refused with the domains listed as the rule and no domain named. `add_validated` is the store's door: an actuator the validator refuses is never added. A named-muscle calibration with provenance widens the domains as a new source packet with its own domain id and version, regenerated into the corpus and the table by the script, not as a code change ([content/muscle](../../content/muscle/README.md#what-a-named-packet-must-contain) lists what the packet must contain).

## The schema
`systems/muscle/schemas/muscle.schema`, namespace `engine.muscle`: the r008 proposal with D21 (5) and the r010 review applied — the units `N`, `J`, `W` (added to schemac's table for this, [schemas](../../schemas/README.md)) so the name suffixes drop; `failed_test` an enumerated `ValidationTest` (every domain, root, event and budget test the law can fail) instead of a string; `FreezeAccountingStatus.Inert = 3`; `TickWork.transition_elastic_jump` the tick's sum; and `LineState.startup_pending`. | Tests and size table | `tests/*_tests.cpp`, `tests/size_table.cpp` | |
| Bench | `bench/muscle_bench.cpp`: `muscle.tick.{step,passive}[.newton]` at 64 and 256 actuators, `muscle.probe` | measured |
| Removal proof | `ENGINE_WITH_MUSCLE`, off in the minimal build | works |

**Removing it.** `cmake --preset msvc-minimal` (or `-DENGINE_WITH_MUSCLE=OFF`) drops the module, its schema library, its tests and its bench; the module disappears from `build/<preset>/modules.json` and is listed there under `disabled_capabilities`.

**Public API.** `include/systems/muscle/muscle.h` (identities, paths), `law.h`, `scheme.h`, `newton.h`, `schedule.h`, `store.h`, `supervisor.h`, `validator.h`.

**Depends on.** `base`, `containers`, `math`, `log`, `ids`, `hash`, `json`, `schema`, `muscle_schemas`.

**Testing.** `tools/dev.ps1 test -Filter muscle`. Benchmarks: `tools/dev.ps1 bench -Preset msvc-release -Filter 'muscle.*'`.

**Performance.** Measured, not met ([the cost](../experiments/muscle-line-tier-cost.md)): **41.6 µs per actuator per tick** for the step case and **46.7 µs** for the stiff passive one (MSVC release, quiet machine), against D20's 2 µs and the owner's estimate of 10 to 50 µs; Brent's root costs 51.7 and 60.6. The time is the residual — six transcendentals and two square roots each, some 200 to 320 residuals an actuator a tick — and the first reductions are four lanes across actuators (the kernel has no early exit a mask cannot express), fewer length-dependent transcendentals along a root, and a better Newton start. No allocation in the tick, by construction: the store's arrays are sized by `reserve` and never grow (`add` refuses past the capacity), a tick's trial — stage states, probe records, work, parent samples — lives on the stack, and its outcome and counters in fixed per-actuator slots; the engine's allocation counters are not reachable from a capability's test, so a reviewer's check is where every buffer lives, which this paragraph lists.
