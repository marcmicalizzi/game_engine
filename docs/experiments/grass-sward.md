# Grass sward: research and acceptance (G88)

Reserved on 2026-10-25 for the sward solver: grass as ground coverage, with blade
patches near the eye, clump cards beyond them, and the ground material far away.
This is new work; G75's isolated tufts remain complete and unchanged.

The solver builds on W72's ground masks and W73's streamed placements. Field-sway
motion, caster policies, placement-written paint and empty far forms are separately
briefed engine work. Their interfaces must be available before the integrated
meadow and forest-clearing acceptance measurements can pass.

Research, implementation and acceptance are in progress. No timing or coverage
result has been measured yet. The owner's visual acceptance remains required.
