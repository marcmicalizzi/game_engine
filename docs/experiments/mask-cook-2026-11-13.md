# The mask level's cook, and the tile set's point read without its lock (T128, 2026-11-13)

What the change bought, as pairs of binaries built from one tree (`msvc-release`, the base 61ccce67 and the branch's tip), on the machine as it was. The design is on [tileset](../subsystems/tileset.md#the-mask-levels-cook) ("The mask level's cook") and "The reader's lock"; the finding this starts from is T125's (the forest's 763 s of cold "placement litter" is this cook, on one thread).

## The scene

T125's 3 km by 3 km engine scene: a 3,073-point square heightfield at 1 m (`engine-content terrain-synth --kind mountain`, 37.8 MB of float32), a `tiles` ground in 64-cell tiles, masks by priors (soil, scree, bedrock, snow) at 1 m in 32-cell tiles — 9,409 mask tiles, 70.8 MB of records — and one litter entry over 16 by 16 tiles of 32 m.

## The cook alone: `engine-content terrain-masks`

`engine-content terrain-masks <scene.json> <out dir> --ddc <root> [--jobs <n>]`, the set already in the root, the level cooked into a fresh directory each run; `ms` is the command's own figure for `ensure_mask_level`. Every run wrote the same archive (SHA-256 `32edabc86fc409e9…`) and the same level hash (`910dfad20f1be587`).
