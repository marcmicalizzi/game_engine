# ADR-0055: The muscle line tier: the adopted scheme, the fixed schedule and the failure semantics

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Plan references:** docs/plan/05-simulation.md §5.16; docs/plan/11-performance-principles.md
- **Docs touched:** `docs/subsystems/muscle.md`, `docs/subsystems/math.md`, `schemas/README.md`, `docs/plan/05-simulation.md` §5.16, `docs/roadmap.md` (C53, C54)

## Context

The engine's muscles are line actuators — a Hill-type fiber with activation dynamics in series with a compliant tendon on a routed path — at the near tier, with a rigid-tendon tier and an inert animation tier behind them (the owner's targets note, 2026-10-11). The law and its integration were qualified offline by an authoring track over ten revisions against a held-control Radau reference on a 39-trajectory corpus (the five original cases, an offset pulse, an active wave and sixteen parameter corners in a step and a passive variant), under four decisions the engine owner confirmed:

- **D20** fixed the targets and the failure accounting: completion; tendon force within `max(1% of the trajectory's peak, 0.5% of F0)`; fiber length within `0.5% lopt`; the trajectory's work residual `R = W_ext + W_active_output - dE - W_diss` within `1e-3` of the reference throughput; a 60 Hz tick with held excitation and at most eight nominal substeps; a failed tick retains the committed activation, fiber length and force cache, emits stale forces, and never catches up; 2 µs per actuator per tick, 200 actuators per character, SoA, no allocation in the tick, the same bytes on MSVC, GCC and Clang.
- **D21** settled the classification (a row with no crossing that passes force and fiber and fails only work is order-limited at eight substeps, and more substeps or a relaxed gate is not its remedy), the validator (model identity, parameter-set identity, a qualified domain with id and version; refusals `domain_unqualified`, `model_unrecognized`, `parameter_outside_domain` naming the field, value, unit and rule; the only qualified domain at first the enumerated synthetic corpus, `synthetic-study-only`), the supervisor's rules, and the schema at registration (units N, J, W; an enumerated `ValidationTest`; `Inert = 3`).
- **D22** recorded r008: a located slack/taut crossing under the unchanged law (Arm A) cleaned up the switch rows' events but left ten rows resolution-limited at eight substeps under SDIRK2; a C1 tendon toe (Arm B) was not adopted.
- **D23** recorded r009: Alexander's three-stage, stiffly accurate, L-stable SDIRK of order three (Arm S) qualified 33 of 39 under the unchanged gates, with four step rows that miss work only (2.9, 3.1, 1.7, 1.8 times the limit: the control step's transient, order-limited at either order) and two passive corners that miss force only at the first nominal endpoint (5.95 and 5.43 N against 5 N: a start-up transient). S with the r008 locator was adopted as tier 0's scheme, and r010 was declared: the first tick from a static start in four pieces per substep, and four equal S steps for a crossing's remainder. The owner's r010 review adopted both refinements as the engine's fixed schedule whatever r010 measures, and fixed the `startup_pending` marker's engine representation.

## Decision

1. **The scheme is r009's S, unchanged**: the r005 law; Alexander SDIRK3 with the pinned binary64 tableau for the mechanics; an independent RK4 for each stage's activation from the probe's starting activation over `c_i d`; the scalar implicit stage root with its bracket, `nextafter` guards and Brent tolerances (`xtol 2e-14`, `rtol 1e-14`, 64 iterations); the five work channels integrated with the scheme's own signed stage weights. A safeguarded Newton inside the same bracket may replace Brent only if the corpus gates hold and the bytes agree across the three compilers (D23 (5)).
2. **The schedule is r010's**: tick `k` advances `[(k-1)/60, k/60]` through eight parents of `H = 1/480 s`, each starting at its canonical time with the state carried; a parent is four pieces of `H/4` while `startup_pending` is set and one piece of `H` otherwise; after each piece's probe the gap `L - projected(l) - Ls` is read at the start, both internal stages and the end; a detected crossing is located by 20 dyadic bisections of the piece's original-start endpoint map, the high side kept, within `h/2^20` and `1e-8 m`; its remainder is four equal S steps; one crossing per parent; at most two zero-boundary derivative queries per parent. The four event codes are `EventUnresolved`, `EventTangentUnresolved`, `EventLocationUnresolved` and `EventBudgetExceeded`. The ceilings are 28 probes per startup parent and 25 otherwise.
3. **Commit or roll back the whole tick.** Success installs activation, fiber length, the cache, `last_commit_tick = k` and `startup_pending = false`, and adds the tick's five work channels. Any failure anywhere retains the committed record bit for bit, adds zero work and reports the actuator, the enumerated test and the value it read; the next tick retries from the retained state with its new inputs. No retry inside a tick, no clamp, no fabricated state. Static initialisation is the only way to arm `startup_pending`; a restore keeps it as saved.
4. **The corpus is the only qualified domain.** The validator refuses an unknown model id, a parameter-set identity outside `synthetic-study-only` version 1 (the 39 rows' distinct identities, enumerated in `content/muscle/`), and a value outside the law's domain. Identities are opaque 32-byte values the engine carries and does not recompute; the engine's own model id is the SHA-256 of a canonical text of its law, committed beside the tests.
5. **The committed bytes are the same on every toolchain**: the law calls only `engine::det` transcendentals (`core/math/deterministic.h`), the tree compiles with contraction off (ADR-0035), and the tests pin a 64-bit hash of every corpus row's final state and work that MSVC, GCC and Clang reproduce.

## Consequences

- The corpus replay is the gate. A trajectory the kernel fails is a test failure with a witness, never a tolerance change; the four step rows and the two first-sample rows are carried as known limits with r009 S's numbers until r010's result review changes the labels, and a labelled row may not get worse than 1.5 times its recorded number.
- The cost is measured, not met: D20 names 2 µs per actuator per tick, and the owner's estimate of this scheme at eight parents and three implicit stages was 10 to 50 µs. The number and where it goes are stage 1's deliverable; the scheme and the substep count do not change to hit it.
- The supervisor (degradation to the rigid tier, the inert tier, freeze accounting, promotion), the scheduler entry, records and the skeleton's path are deferred to stage 2; the schema declares their fields now so the committed record does not change shape when they arrive.
- A change to the law, the tableau, the schedule, the gates or the qualified domain is an authoring-side declaration and a new ADR, not an engine edit.

## Revisit when

r010's result review changes the known-limit labels; a named-muscle calibration with provenance arrives to widen the qualified domain; the measured cost is found to need a scheme change (which would go back to the authoring side); or stage 2's supervisor finds a failure rate on real skeletal paths that the schedule cannot carry.
