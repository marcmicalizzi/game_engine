# ADR-0080: Water carries a state, and the full equations are what every form of it is held to

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Plan references:** docs/plan/05-simulation.md §5.15 (the capability inventory's "Fluids and water surfaces"), docs/plan/11-performance-principles.md §11.10 (absent capabilities are free)
- **Docs touched:** `docs/subsystems/water_state.md` ("The design: a state in a world"), `docs/roadmap.md` (W173 and its stages W191 to W198)

## Context

Every water the engine draws today is a function of the clock and of fields built once at load ([water-state-grounding](../experiments/water-state-grounding.md#what-has-no-state-today)): a channel is the steady solve's level and speed at its stations, a body is a level over a flooded hollow, the surf's foam is a sum over a periodic train, a stream's white is the solve's Froude number read per pixel. There is no depth or velocity that anything can change, no ground store that fills, and no record of where energy was lost.

The owner asked for the opposite (roadmap W173, 2026-11-24 and 2026-11-25): a source placed in the map fills down, a dam fills until it overflows, the bed takes water until it is saturated, white water is "a consequence of turbulent water as opposed to a texture to be drawn overtop of the water, the same on streams and on waves", and the sea answers the wind, the tide and the seabed. The order is the owner's too: "the first big step to realism on it is getting the elements of simulating it right, then cutting it down for performance and shortcuts", with baked, real-time and a mix of the two as a setting of the game's. The design must leave room for what the water does to its ground (W178 to W183) and for a game's own time scales (W187).

What was on the table, from the grounding page's reading of the published models:

- **More closed forms** (a fill animation, a dam's effect, a better foam function). Each is a new function of the clock with nothing shared between them; none is a consequence of the others, which is the owner's complaint about the white caps.
- **The pipe model** (columns exchanging water through virtual pipes). Cheap and never negative, and shipped in games; it has no advection of momentum, so a stream does not carry into a lake, a bore does not form, and breaking cannot be the equations' own.
- **The cheaper waves of hydrology.** The local inertial approximation cannot dry a wet bed; the diffusion wave's explicit step collapses on flat water and holds no jump; the kinematic wave cannot pool, and under constant inflow it is the steady solve the engine already has.
- **The full shallow-water equations**, by a well-balanced, positivity-preserving finite-volume step. The only family that holds a jump, a pool's backwater and a drying front together and keeps a lake at rest. By the grounding page's table a stream and a pool on decimetre cells take one step a 60 Hz frame; under the reference's own, stricter step rule a surf zone out to deep water and a fall's foot take two (*derived* on the design's page). Published at 530 million cells a second on a 2010 card in single precision.
- **Three-dimensional particles or grids.** What a height field cannot hold (a nappe, an overturning crest, spray) needs them; nothing the owner listed does, and the engine's water is drawn as a surface over a bed.

## Decision

1. **Water has a state wherever a game asks for one.** A cell carries depth and the two components of depth-averaged momentum, the water its ground holds, and a small set of carried scalars. The state moves by the full shallow-water equations with bed friction, sources and the ground's take; no term is dropped in the form everything else is held to.

2. **The reference is the full equations on the CPU, in double precision** (W184, module `water_state`): no renderer, no card, the same bytes on every toolchain and at any thread count, held by cases with closed-form answers. It is first order in space to begin with and gains second order once its first-order cases pass; whatever is judged says which it was. Its first night showed one thing it does not yet hold, a steady flow over a bed that steps between cells (W199), and nothing is tied to a spill's level until it does. **Every other form of the state is held to the reference by cases with stated bounds**: the step on the card (W175), each tier, and each shortcut. A shortcut that cannot state its error against it is not taken.

3. **The simulation comes first and whole; shortcuts come after, one switch each.** The first form that runs in a scene is the reference's own step on the card, in single precision throughout, at the reference's own step rule, on its grid; nothing else is approximated. Each later economy (a smaller number type, a coarser far grid, a step every other tick, a ceiling on the sub-steps a tick may take, a cheaper wave where it is provably enough) is a tunable of its own that is off until the owner has judged it by eye, standing alone; a perceptual measure (T232) says where to look and is never the judge.

4. **Tiers are a setting of the game's: baked, live, and mixed.** *Baked* is what the engine draws today: the steady solve and the closed forms, no state, no cost. *Live* runs the state in every domain the scene declares, within range of an observer. *Mixed* draws baked water everywhere and runs the state only in tiles that something has woken: an edit, a source that changed, or an observer of the simulation coming within range of a domain the game marks. **Waking and retiring follow the simulation's observer set with its hysteresis ([ADR-0010](0010-deterministic-sim-and-lod-contract.md)), never the render camera**, so which tiles are awake is the simulation's own state. A tile starts from the baked answer and retires to it when its state has settled back within a stated bound; a tile that has not settled retires only where no observer can see it. The baked answer is therefore three things at once: the far field beyond every domain, the start of every tile, and the check a settled tile is compared with (W176).

5. **The bed's height is a state and never a constant of the solver.** A step takes the bed as a field and may find it changed since the last step; nothing derived from the bed is kept between steps; where the bed moves under water each cell keeps its depth, so volume is conserved and the surface moves. The bed a step sees is the built ground plus a bounded mobile layer over it. **The ground owns its layers** (W183; stone gives no mobile layer): an awake tile works on a copy of the layer's thickness that it took when it woke, and hands the copy back when it retires and when a save is written, through a registration point, neither module naming the other. The bed's roughness is an input of the same kind, since a rippled bed is rougher than a flat one (W178's grounding). The bed moves on a clock of its own, coarser than the water's, because its waves travel far slower.

6. **Carried quantities ride one transport path.** Beside depth and momentum a cell carries an array of scalars moved by the same mass fluxes, conservatively and without new extrema, each with its own source, decay and exchange with the bed. The foam is the first: its source is the energy lost at the faces the step identifies as a bore or a jump (the jump's own head loss times the flux through it, [grounding §4](../experiments/water-state-grounding.md#4-breaking-as-something-simulated)), gated by the speed that entrains air, and it fades at fresh or salt water's rate; it is never whatever a scheme smears on a smooth slope. Suspended sediment is the next (W178): one or two grain classes whose source and sink are an exchange with the bed's mobile layer, so what settles raises the bed and what is lifted lowers it, and the pair is counted together. The step does not know which scalar is which; a scene carries as many as its capabilities registered.

7. **Whatever changes the water enters on the world clock, and a replay carries it.** A scene's domains and its authored sources are the scene's data. What happens while a session runs (a source's rate changed, a dam, a body lying in the flow as a raised bed or a blocked share of its cells (W181), a disturbance of the bed) is an event with a tick, applied between steps in a fixed order. A replay carries the inflows, the events, the observers' moves and the calendar's transitions, and repeats the state on the same device.

8. **The state gives out what its readers need, with no second solve.** Per cell: the depth and the level, the velocity, the stress at the bed, and the rate of dissipation; and where a domain resolves waves, a running measure of the to-and-fro at the bed (the orbital speed and excursion the bed's rows read, W179 and W180), which the wave field's closed forms give where no state runs. Drawing reads the level, the depth and the foam; the bed reads the stress and the speed; plants read the speed (W182); a body reads the pressure and the flow past it as forces (W181), through bridging code in `systems/`.

9. **Three clocks, and each part of the state reads one** (W187, [ADR-0056](0056-one-world-clock-and-the-days-length.md)). What moves in front of the eye is on **the world clock**: depth, momentum, the foam, a front on a beach, the ground filling under a stream and draining near its surface, scour round a fresh obstacle. The tide's level at a surf zone's seaward edge is a closed form of **the sky's hour** and the date. What is slow reads **the calendar** and is **given at a date, never integrated through a gap**: a stream's seasonal inflow, the water table and the ground's deep drying, a shore's section and a river's long bed (a baked family or a closed form by date). When the calendar jumps, every awake tile starts again from the steady answer for the new date, which is the path a tile already takes when it wakes; the ground's store and the mobile layer take the date's values, except where a game has marked an edit as lasting. **An event's strength is its forcing's** (a hurricane's waves, surge and wind, within the bounds the forcing laws were fitted to); the bed's own quickening stays low during an event, since the published factor assumes a response linear in the forcing and storms break that (W178's grounding).

10. **Absent is free, present is counted, and the state steps with the simulation.** A scene that declares no domain, or runs baked, adds no pass, makes no pool and adds no branch to the resolve ([plan 11 §11.10](../plan/11-performance-principles.md#1110-absent-capabilities-are-free)); the "nothing enabled" frame does not move, and a tile's own material says whether it is awake, so no pixel asks. A tile's pools are made when it first wakes and are reported by tag; a scene's declared domains give its worst-case live tiles at load, so launch memory is a number the load prints. **The state advances by the simulation's ticks, not by frames**: a frame submits the steps its ticks owe (none, one or two), and anything read back from the card arrives a fixed number of ticks later. A host with no device runs baked, or the reference's arithmetic over a small domain where a game asks for it. The step, the moving surface, the traced structures' rebuild and the resolve's reads each take a line in the frame budgets ([ADR-0018](0018-frame-budgets-as-merge-gates.md)) once W175 has measured the step.

11. **The determinism stance is stated in ADR-0027's terms.** The reference is the same bytes everywhere, and a state stepped by its arithmetic inside the tick may be `hashed`. **The state on the card is `derived` until measurement shows it can be more, and nothing `hashed` reads it.** On one device it is the same bytes twice: a fixed order; a face's flux computed once and stored, or twice in one fixed operand order, so both sides of a face agree to the bit and water is conserved exactly; every reduction (the step's limit, the ledger's sums) in a fixed tree order with no atomic; contraction forbidden where it matters; cell-local positions ([ADR-0053](0053-world-positions-are-f64-and-the-gpu-sees-none.md)). Whether it can also be the same bytes on every conformant device (addition, subtraction and multiplication are correctly rounded there; division and the square root are not, and would have to be built from the first three; denormals must behave alike) is a question W175 answers by measurement. The game's question about the water at a point keeps today's guarantee, the same bits on every toolchain, because it answers from `hashed` sources only; a second, presentation-only question may read the card's state and says so.

12. **Where it lives.** The reference is `domain/water_state`, a capability with no dependency on a renderer or another capability. The state in a running scene is a second capability in `systems/` (`water_live`) that owns the domains, their tiles and pools, the scheduler's entry that advances them, the step's compute pass (in the render graph only while a tile is awake) and the events; it is held to the reference through that module's cases. Drawing stays the water capability's and the renderer's: they read the state through a registration point, as they read a body's depth map today. Each can be removed from the build without the others noticing (ADR-0027).

## Consequences

- The steady solve, the plan's lakes and the open sea's spectrum are not replaced: they become the far field, the start and the check. Work on them (a lake's outlet as a control, W160; the surf's closed forms, W174) keeps its value in every tier, and is the whole of the water in the baked one.
- Several closed forms become consequences inside an awake tile and must be switched off there, one by one, as the state takes each over: the swash's ballistic sheet, the wet sand's drain, a stream's white by Froude number, the bore foam, the still body's level. The page says which, and the order is the stages'.
- A surface that moves means the surface's mesh, the depth the resolve reads and the traced structures take the state each tick, and a moving surface's bounds and level of detail cannot be the ones a cluster hierarchy fitted to a rest pose. That is the larger half of the cost of seeing anything, and it is budgeted with the step, not after it.
- A grid cannot hold the sea or a whole river. Domains are bounded, and every seam is specified before it is built: a domain against the far field (what is counted, and what spends the count), a fine tile against a coarse one (the flux summed over the fine side's sub-steps, one height datum for the whole domain), a surf corridor against the sea (an edge that generates the incoming train and lets the reflected one leave, which the reference does not have yet).
- An explicit step's cost follows the water: cells times the sub-steps the stability limit asks times the world's rate. The first form pays that honestly and reports it; a ceiling on it is a shortcut.
- A slow exchange can fall under a single-precision field's resolution (a loam's take in one step is smaller than the last bit of a metre of depth), so the ground's take and the bed's change are applied on a coarser clock or through a compensated sum. W175 holds both by cases before anything leans on them.
- On steep ground the equations' own assumptions weaken and a decimetre grid resolves neither a step-pool stream's jets nor its clasts. Such reaches stay baked and the state runs in their pools; that is a limit of the model, stated on the page with what has and has not been read about it.
- Forbidden from here on: a solver constant that stands for the bed; a foam that is not fed by the state inside an awake tile; a shortcut with no switch or with no stated error against the reference; a tier that costs anything when a scene does not use it; anything `hashed` reading a `derived` state.

## Decisions that are the owner's, each with a recommendation

1. **What gameplay's water is.** The engine's rule is that nothing the simulation hashes may read a per-device result. So either gameplay reads the baked answer and, where a game asks for live water it can feel, a coarse state stepped inside the tick by the reference's arithmetic (the same on every machine), with the card's finer state as the picture and held to the coarse one; or gameplay reads the card's state and such a game gives up machine-independent replays. *Recommended*: the first, until W175 shows whether the card's step can be the same bytes on every device; if it can, one state serves both and the question disappears.
2. **How long filling takes.** At the world's own rate a pond behind a dam fills over hours ([grounding §2](../experiments/water-state-grounding.md#2-a-source-that-fills-downhill-a-dam-that-fills-and-overflows)). *Recommended*: the physics runs at the world clock's rate; a game that wants a pond full sooner gives its source more water, which is the game's world to author, and the engine offers no hidden multiplier on a domain's clock.
3. **What a save carries.** *Recommended*: the events always; the ground's layers and the store where a domain is marked persistent; depth and momentum only for a domain whose far field is stale (decision 8), until the background solve has replaced it, and never otherwise, since a tile that wakes starts from the steady answer and settles.
4. **Steep streams stay baked, with live pools.** *Recommended*: yes, by the steady solve's own classing of step-pool and cascade reaches; the slope at which the equations' hydrostatic assumption fails was not read (two sources reached only as summaries), and reading them comes before any number is fixed.
5. **The surf starts without dispersion.** The shallow-water equations break every wave as a bore, which is right in the inner surf and early offshore of it; Boussinesq-type equations break later and cost more a cell. *Recommended*: the shallow-water corridor first, with its seaward edge set where the water is shallow against the wavelength, judged by eye, and dispersion only if the picture asks for it.
6. **The default tier.** *Recommended*: baked, for every scene that says nothing, so no scene changes until a game opts in; the live tier is judged standing alone before any scene ships with it.
7. **The fastest world rate a live state follows.** Every doubling of the world's rate doubles the steps a tick. *Recommended*: a domain follows the world clock up to a stated rate (four times, to start); past it a domain is retired to the baked answer for as long as the rate lasts, and a domain whose far field is stale is frozen as it stands instead.
8. **An edit the bake does not know** (a dam the player built). Its far field is wrong until the steady solve is run again over the reach it changed. *Recommended*: run that reach's steady solve again in the background on the job system and adopt it as the new far field; until it is ready the domain is marked stale, stays awake or is frozen as it stands when no observer sees it, and its depth is saved.
9. **What marks water as breaking, and at what order the first form is judged.** The foam's source needs a test for the faces where energy is truly lost: the jump the scheme itself resolves (a face where the flow passes from shooting to tranquil), a criterion on how fast the surface rises (Kennedy's, with its published constants), or a test on the front's steepness. And a first-order step smears a front over several cells, which a second-order one does not. *Recommended*: the scheme's own jump as the source, gated by the surface-rise criterion so a steep but unbroken wave makes no foam; and the reference mended at a bed step (W199) and taken to second order before the first picture is judged, so that what the owner sees is the equations and not the scheme's smear.

## Revisit when

- W175's measurement puts the full step outside the frame at the grid a lake, a stream reach or a beach needs: the order "full first" stands, but the first shortcut is then chosen before a scene can run live.
- The first live scene shows a seam between a tile and the far field that the eye finds: the mixed tier's retirement bound, or the tier itself, is reopened.
- W175 shows the card's step is the same bytes on every device: decision 11's `derived` is lifted and the owner's decision 1 is settled by it.
- The bed's rows (W178 to W180) need more from the step than the bed as an input, the stress and the wave's measure as outputs and a carried scalar with an exchange: decision 5, 6 or 8 is reopened before any of them is worked around.
